# Staged story composition studies

These are concept images made with the built-in image generation tool. Each shows one illustration partway through its reveal: completed areas carry player-applied watercolor, while future areas remain pen-and-ink linework. They are visual studies, not screenshots or final game assets.

| Study | How the story grows | Possible colour-and-water puzzle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Branching flower](branching-flower.png) | A first flower sends a stem into leaves, buds, and a moth; each new line grows from an earlier one. | Mix or find the colour needed for a newly revealed leaf. |
| [Moth journey](moth-journey.png) | A moth leaves the flower, the eye follows a curling line and stream, and the camera pans toward a moonlit pond. | Guide pigment along a wet path, then use a colour clue in the next scene. |
| [Hidden moth](hidden-moth.png) | Small flowers, leaves, stars, and curls accumulate until they resolve into one large moth. | Reuse a colour or pattern learned in an earlier detail to complete another part of the wings. |

The separate prompts called for warm paper, confident but imperfect pen lines with varied pressure and hatching, selective indigo-violet watercolor, unpainted future elements, and no text, numbers, panels, UI, or paint-by-number outlines. The first prompt emphasized branching growth from one flower; the second, a horizontal journey discovered by panning; the third, a distant composition revealed by accumulated small details.

The intended line animation would trace each new contour along its pen path. The colour transition in these stills suggests stage order; it does not depict an actual animation.

## Awakening sequence

Three built-in image-generation edits of the same [side-on layered storybook study](../StyleStudies/layered-storybook-depth.png) show a proposed visual progression:

1. [Linework only](awakening-1-linework.png): ink drawing on warm paper, with little depth or light.
2. [Adding colour](awakening-2-painting.png): wet pigment moves from the flower through the ground and pond, while the right side remains mostly linework.
3. [Coming to life](awakening-3-alive.png): the whole scene gains colour, a glowing lantern, lit windows, moving petals, water ripples, and stronger separation between foreground, playable plane, and distant scenery.

These are still frames. The repeated composition illustrates art direction, but individual contours vary slightly between generated edits; they are not animation frames or production layers.
