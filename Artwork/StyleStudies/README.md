# Colourbug art style studies

The first three concept edits use [the same branching-flower composition](../StoryStudies/branching-flower.png) as a reference, so differences in line, colour, and mood are easier to compare. The adventure game study uses [the moth journey](../StoryStudies/moth-journey.png). They are visual studies made with the built-in image generation tool, not game assets.

| Study | Line | Colour and mood | Possible fit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Whispering ink](whispering-ink.png) | Sparse, broken hairlines and open contours. | Pale washes, generous paper, dreamlike quiet. | Keeps the player’s flowing paint prominent. |
| [Folk storybook](folk-storybook.png) | Bold, uneven contours and simple rounded shapes. | Dusty blue, plum, coral, and a small warm glow; cosy mystery. | Strong readability at iPad size and an inviting tone. |
| [Ink nocturne](ink-nocturne.png) | Brush-pen lines that swell into dark ink and taper away. | Moonlit indigo washes against luminous paper; gentle drama. | Gives the staged line reveal a strong presence. |
| [Adventure game](adventure-game.png) | Illustrated contours and detailed scenery, with future areas left as pen-and-ink linework. | Rich sunset forest, a winding stream, and a moonlit destination. | Makes each reveal feel like entering a new place, with environmental clues to discover. |
| [Platform adventure](platform-adventure.png) | Clear, side-on ground and ledges; distant silhouettes and foreground leaves form separate flat planes. | Colour travels from a vivid flower toward an unpainted pond and lantern. | Supports lateral movement, staged reveals, and paint flowing ahead of the player. |

The shared edit prompt preserved the lower painted flower, growing stems, buds, moth, and unpainted future linework. Each variant then changed the line quality, level of detail, and palette treatment while keeping an organic ink-and-wash feel. No text, UI, hands, or new story subjects were requested.

The adventure game edit preserved the flower and moth at left, winding stream in the middle, and moonlit pond at right. Its prompt called for a classic illustrated point-and-click adventure background with layered depth, atmospheric dusk, readable places and clues, and a small lantern-shaped seedpod by the pond. The left is coloured, the middle partly revealed, and the right awaits the player's colour. No interface, pixel art, or text was requested.

The platform adventure study revises the depth model: a fixed side-on camera, horizontal playable ground with small ledges and a shallow pool, distant silhouette backdrop, and a few foreground overlaps. Colour and linework still reveal the journey from left to right. Its prompt explicitly excluded perspective paths, a receding river, and a vanishing point.

## Side-on style variations

These four generated studies hold the moth, flower, horizontal route, pool, and lantern as recurring story elements. Each explores a different visual language for a flat 2D platform adventure. The paper theatre and moonlit ink studies were generated from composition prompts; animated storybook and folk-art night garden were edited from the [platform adventure study](platform-adventure.png).

| Study | Art direction | Gameplay idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Paper theatre](paper-theatre.png) | Layered cut-paper silhouettes, fibrous edges, and shallow cast shadows. | Colour might soak through paper or reveal a hidden layer. |
| [Moonlit ink](moonlit-ink.png) | Sparse sumi-like brush marks, wide negative space, and bleeding ink washes. | The flowing pigment is the main character; each new mark changes a quiet scene. |
| [Animated storybook](animated-storybook.png) | Rounded expressive plants, bold loose contours, and warm gouache-like colour. | Painted plants could visibly stretch, sway, and respond. |
| [Folk-art night garden](folk-night-garden.png) | Flat decorative leaves and flowers, repeated motifs, and jewel tones. | Painting could complete patterns that wake paths and creatures. |

All four use a coloured left side that transitions into unpainted linework at the right. They are concept images, not implementation-ready layered assets.

## Layered storybook synthesis

[Layered storybook](layered-storybook.png) combines the shallow drop-shadow depth of separated paper planes, a sweet hand-drawn moth, and richer wet watercolour with varied pigment. The planes are illustrations painted on paper rather than flat coloured-paper cutouts. The side-on route remains readable; the right side waits as open ink linework. The distant castle silhouettes add a more fantastic setting. This is a visual direction study, not a ready-to-use game scene.

[Layered storybook with depth](layered-storybook-depth.png) revises that study with softer, paler distant scenery, a distinct dark foreground foliage plane, and the illustrated ground extending to the bottom of the frame. It removes the exposed lower edge and blank space that made the play surface look like a floating paper strip.

[Layered storybook with shadows](layered-storybook-shadows.png) restores soft offset cast shadows behind individual foreground leaves and flowers, giving the illustrated planes a clearer hand-layered paper feel while keeping the ground full bleed to the bottom edge.
